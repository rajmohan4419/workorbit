# Invoice engine v2 — 15 November 2026

Implemented on `feat/invoice-engine-upgrade`, based on `5819860` (the foundation work in PR #243). No merge, deployment, production schema application, live payment activation or new price was performed. No AGENTS.md exists in the checkout or applicable parent directories. No reference PDFs were attached to this request; the written field requirements were sufficient. Templates use original OrbitBoard layout and styling.

## Architecture and behavior

- The existing GST identity/checksum engine and bundled Inter fonts are reused. Legacy calculation/PDF entry points remain compatible with v1 callers; the existing tool route now exposes `InvoiceWorkspace`. Its v2 model, validation and minor-unit calculator are shared by browser preview and the server PDF renderer.
- Five original A4 presentations: Modern Business (default, violet), Classic Ledger (rules and paired address columns), Compact Professional (dense grayscale), Commercial Export (paired seller/sold-to and delivery), Detailed Shipping (denser transport/package sections). All support every supported invoice type; template changes never change tax treatment or discard particulars. No fixed filler rows. Item headers repeat on continuation pages. Summary height is measured to keep totals, currency words and signature together. Dates use ISO format, dates align right with Due directly beneath Date, and blank Due/optional fields are omitted.
- Separate B2B/B2C/export modes. B2B requires a valid locally checked GSTIN and matching state code. B2C renders `GSTIN: NA` and requires recipient/delivery particulars at the ₹50,000 taxable-value threshold or on recipient request. Registered buyers must use B2B. Seller address, state, city, valid PIN and GST registration are mandatory. Bill of supply/unregistered-seller modes are unsupported and blocked.
- Searchable local country/state choices and state-filtered city suggestions use pinned MIT-licensed `countrycitystatejson@26.11.1001`, with country-level lazy city loading; postal format checks use `postal-codes-js@2.5.2`. No external location API. Statutory Indian state names/codes take precedence over legacy dataset spellings; missing suggestions (including some recently reorganized areas) permit manual city entry. Foreign provinces/localities allow manual entry. Country and postal validation are separate from Indian PIN rules. Foreign Tax ID/VAT is distinct from GSTIN. Sold-to and ship-to remain separate, with an explicit same-as-billing option. Different importer details are available for export goods.
- Export goods/services are distinct. User must review statutory eligibility and select LUT/bond without IGST or eligible IGST payment; a foreign address alone never establishes export eligibility. LUT reference/date/validity review or specific notification/eligibility review is required. IEC or an exemption explanation is required for export goods. Export goods can carry packages, weights with units, origin, Incoterms, ports, freight/insurance/transport references and importer details; services omit these goods-only fields. Domestic goods can carry optional delivery/package/transport fields. Freight/insurance amounts belong in classified charge lines with explicit tax-treatment review; pure-agent treatment is unsupported.
- Quantities/rates support six decimal places, discounts/GST/ad-valorem cess two places, using BigInt-scaled decimal multiplication and half-up rounding to minor units. Component taxes round independently. INR final invoice total rounds to whole rupees with visible round-off; supported foreign currencies keep two decimals. Payments received are captured at finalization and balance due is calculated. Post-finalization payment tracking is not advertised as implemented.
- Foreign invoice currency and INR reporting are separate. Supported currency subset: INR, USD, EUR, GBP, AED, AUD, CAD and SGD (all two-decimal invoice units). The user supplies and records the reviewed time-of-supply rate, source and date under Rule 34. GST report values convert rounded invoice components individually to INR. No live FX fetch, customs exchange-rate determination, accounting certification or implicit export qualification is provided.

## Persistence, quota and billing

- Local v2 drafts persist in browser storage. Existing local seller fields are recovered when starting v2 without a current draft. Sign-in uses the existing `DownloadAccountForm`/Supabase auth and retains the current draft. Existing `business_profiles` can populate seller details; saving a seller reuses the existing `create_business_profile` RPC and stores only the fields supported by that schema.
- Free template selection/preview does not require an account. Default selection requires sign-in and is stored per user and invoice type in `invoice_preferences`; account/default restoration preserves other entered fields.
- `api/invoices.js` authenticates every account operation with the existing server identity helper. It validates all invoice particulars, generates the PDF on the server with bundled fonts, and only then invokes the service-only atomic `finalize_invoice` RPC. No client-provided user ID, paid flag or quota is authoritative.
- Configurable launch policy: **Free = five lifetime finalizations; Pro/Business = unlimited at launch**. These are allowance assumptions, not new prices. `invoice_policy` is authoritative, with `finalization_limit` and `reset_policy` (`lifetime`/`monthly`) per tier. Changing the policy is a separate reviewed configuration operation. Paid tier comes from the existing trusted `user_subscriptions` table: active recognized Pro/Business code and unexpired period. Existing Razorpay/payment infrastructure is preserved; no second checkout is added. Invoice checkout is explicitly unavailable/contact support; no live price or purchase button is activated.
- Previews/drafts/edits/default selection are unmetered. A transaction takes an account advisory lock, checks idempotency before quota, validates the current allowance, inserts the immutable snapshot/PDF and audit event, and increments usage together. Database failures roll everything back. Failed PDF generation never reaches the debit. Duplicate clicks/concurrent requests cannot exceed the allowance. Same-request retries are free; a changed-payload retry is rejected. Invoice numbers are unique for a seller GSTIN and financial year, case-insensitively. This protects invoices issued through this system; numbering continuity/uniqueness across external books remains the issuer’s responsibility.
- Saved originals are immutable. Re-download uses stored bytes and does not consult current paid entitlement or consume invoice credits. Downloading another template renders the same saved particulars and records a download event, without changing the saved original. Changed particulars require a new invoice/request/number. History is searchable and paginated, so older invoices remain reachable after downgrade/exhaustion.
- RLS and grants isolate snapshots, preferences, usage and audit events. Only the server role can finalize or append audit events; authenticated clients cannot insert/update/delete snapshots or alter quotas. Preferences enforce owner `USING` and `WITH CHECK`. Service role stays server-only. `src/lib/invoices/tiers.js` centralizes the invoice tier/capability contract; saved customers/items, ongoing payment tracking, reminders, bulk invoicing and teams are explicitly unsupported rather than advertised.
- Finalized invoice downloads use the invoice authorization boundary and do not also consume the unrelated generic prepaid PDF balance. Other tools and their existing billing/account flows are unchanged. Privacy and tool guide now distinguish local drafts from explicitly saved invoice particulars/PDF/audit data. Market Lab and the unreleased Business Portal remain hidden.

## Verified legal basis and scope

Rule configuration records verification date **2026-11-15** and official links in `src/lib/invoices/model.js` (`RULES`). This is a constrained invoice preparer, not complete statutory compliance/filing or classification advice.

- CGST Rule 46: supplier/recipient particulars, date, number (1–16 permitted characters, financial-year uniqueness), description, HSN/SAC, goods quantity/unit, value/discount, taxes, explicit place/delivery, reverse-charge indication, signature/electronic-issue exception, and export endorsement. [CBIC consolidated rules](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/pdf/amended-01012022-CGST-Rules-2017-Part-A.pdf); [CBIC current Rule 46 index](https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content-page/explore-rules/1000136/1000001). The index was intermittently inaccessible; the official consolidated provision and official notification/advisory sources were cross-checked. No exhaustive certification of every later amendment is claimed.
- HSN digits under Notification 78/2020-CT, effective 1 April 2021: four up to ₹5 crore preceding-year turnover; six above it; specified B2C relaxation exists. This release conservatively requires HSN/SAC even in a potentially relaxed B2C case. [Official notification](https://gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/2024-05/notfctn-78-central-tax-english-2020.pdf).
- Explicit place of supply must be reviewed under the applicable IGST provisions; it is never determined solely from buyer address. Only ordinary forward charge is supported. Reverse-charge, SEZ and other special supply workflows are blocked, not silently treated as ordinary sales.
- Rule 46 export endorsement uses the exact amended combined statutory wording, including the SEZ phrase in that wording. This does **not** introduce a SEZ option. LUT/bond and IGST endorsements differ. Eligibility is not inferred from address or from printing the endorsement. [Official Rule 46 amendment history in the consolidated rules](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/pdf/amended-01012022-CGST-Rules-2017-Part-A.pdf).
- Export IGST payment depends on the exporter/supply and applicable notifications/exclusions (including Notification 27/2023-CT). The user must record a reviewed notification and eligibility confirmation; the application does not independently adjudicate product exclusions or refund eligibility. [CBIC customs official implementation notice](https://delhicustoms.gov.in/files/air-cargo-export/PN_27_2023.pdf).
- Rule 34 conversion: Customs Act section 14 Board-notified rate for goods, GAAP rate for services, at the relevant time of supply. The entered basis is explicitly reviewed and recorded. [CBIC rules](https://cbic-gst.gov.in/pdf/amended-01012022-CGST-Rules-2017-Part-A.pdf).
- Covered e-invoice cohort generally exceeds ₹5 crore in any covered FY since 2017–18, effective 1 August 2023, subject to notified supplier/supply exemptions. User records historical turnover category and applicability; an above-threshold covered case cannot finalize without a recorded statutory exemption. Required/uncertain IRN or B2C dynamic-QR cases are blocked. No IRN/statutory QR is invented. For AATO ₹10 crore or above, reporting is limited to 30 days from invoice date effective 1 April 2025. [Official IRP notifications](https://einvoice6.gst.gov.in/content/notifications/); [official reporting advisory](https://einvoice6.gst.gov.in/content/revised-time-limit-for-e-invoice-reporting-for-businesses-with-aato-of-%E2%82%B910-crores-above/).
- Electronic issuance requires an explicit IT Act exception confirmation; otherwise an authorised signature image is required. A typed name is not represented as a digital certificate/signing service.
